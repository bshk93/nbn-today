SAC's 2028 1st-round pick: what the trade record shows

Memo2026-01

FromLeague Office
ToAll teams — SAC, MIA, MEM, MIL, DAL and CHA in particular

Five 2028 first-round picks are tied together by swap rights: SAC's, DAL's, MIA's, CHA's and PHX's. Our records of who holds what disagree with each other, and three questions need a ruling from the league before they can be fixed.

Every trade below is quoted from the league's transaction ledger.

Where each pick stands, going by the trades alone

  • DAL's pick: MIL holds it (DAL → NYK → MIL). Three older rights sit on it:
    • SAC's swap right from Trade 78.
    • MEM's swap right from Trade 51.
    • The MIL/DAL swap against CHA's pick from Trades 53 and 31, where MIL takes the better pick and DAL the worse.
  • CHA's pick: DAL holds it, subject to that same MIL/DAL swap.
  • MIA's pick: CHA holds it (MIA → MIN → CHA), subject to SAC's swap right from Trade 15.
  • PHX's pick: MIA holds it outright from Trade 57.
  • SAC's pick: SAC kept it, with swap rights on DAL's and MIA's picks, until the draft-day deal.

The three open questions

  1. What did the draft-day SAC–MIA deal convey?
    • The description says MIA gets "swap rights" and the best of SAC/DAL/PHX. The same entry also lists SAC's 2028 1st as going to MIA outright. Those are different things.
    • "SAC receives 2nd" could mean the second-best pick of the three, or a 2nd-round pick.
    • Nothing says who gets the third pick in that pool.
  2. Does MEM's swap right on DAL's pick (Trade 51) still stand?
    • No later trade in the ledger cancels it.
    • The league sheet still lists it: "MEM can swap its own for the lesser of DAL's and SAC's."
    • The site's pick records don't show MEM anywhere.
  3. Does SAC's swap right on MIA's pick (Trade 15) still stand after the draft-day deal?
    • CHA holds that pick now, so the answer decides whether CHA's pick can be swapped away.

Who is affected

SAC, MIA, MEM, MIL, DAL and CHA all have something riding on the answers. PHX does not, since it traded its pick outright in Trade 57.

What could not be verified

  • Trades 1–9 were entered into the ledger in July 2026 from the original trade write-ups, so their pick terms exist only as the quoted text.
  • The league sheet's position above is taken from a Rosters committee note dated September 24, 2026, not re-read from the sheet itself.

What happens next

Once the league answers the three questions, the pick will be restructured on the site to match. Until then, these six teams may see their 2028 first-round picks shown incorrectly on their team pages.
