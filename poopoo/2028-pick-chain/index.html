The Open Question

For “DAL” to belong in the June 2026 pool, someone would have had to hand MIA (or SAC) a claim on Branch A’s final floor pick — the one DAL only keeps after losing to both MEM and CHA/MIL. No transaction does this.

  • The June 2026 trade’s own structured data moves exactly one pick asset: SAC’s own. DAL is not a party to that trade.
  • The only transaction anywhere touching DAL’s 2028 1st is Trade 31 (2026-07-10) — three weeks after the pool trade, and it’s the CHA-side re-trade, not a transfer to MIA/SAC.
  • Either an off-ledger understanding never got logged as its own trade, or the “best of SAC/DAL/PHX” description is imprecise about what DAL refers to.
