Every trade in league history, graded by what each side actually produced afterward.

How these grades are calculated

Each side of a trade is credited with the total Game Score that the players it acquired went on to produce for that team, regular season and playoffs combined.

  • Production counts from the trade's effective season onward. A trade made after a season's final game counts from the following season.
  • Because season stats are stored per team, a player traded midseason only brings his new team's games with him — production before the trade is never credited.
  • Draft picks are listed but carry no value unless they have already been used and the resulting player is unambiguous. Most traded picks are for future drafts, so they show as pending.
  • Trades whose effective season hasn't produced games yet are marked Too early rather than scored.
  • Cash, exceptions, and swap rights aren't valued.

Grade thresholds come from the actual distribution of margins across all graded trades: Even below the median, Edge to the 75th percentile, Lopsided to the 95th, Fleecing above it.
