Rookie Scale

First-round rookie contract amounts by pick (§ 7.1) — four years, with Years 3 and 4 as team options, plus the § 3.10 RFA cap hold the deal rolls into. The scale indexes annually with the salary cap. Second-round picks are not on the scale; they sign off the minimum scale (§ 3.12) via the Second Round Exception.

A committee token is required to edit these values.
