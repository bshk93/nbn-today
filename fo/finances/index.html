Franchise figures, in NB¥ for flavor. Not spendable, not a balance, and not the Wall Street share price. Every number is made up from real results.

Arena naming rights

Name any team's arena, yours or a rival's. Bids are real NB¥ and the price only goes up: the next bid has to beat the last by 10%. If someone outbids you, the NB¥ you paid is gone. The name shows on that team's box scores from the day you buy it.

Owners

Each season goes to whoever owned the team on January 1. "Beat the league" is how much more (or less) the franchise gained than the league average did over the same seasons, so it adds up to zero across every owner and a long run isn't rewarded just for lasting.
