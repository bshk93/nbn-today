How it works

  • NBN uses the NBA's lottery. From the 2027 draft that is the 3-2-1 format: 16 teams, 37 balls, and every one of the first 16 picks is drawn.
  • The ten teams that miss the play-in get 3 balls each, except the three worst records, which get 2. The 9 and 10 seeds get 2 each. The two 7-vs-8 play-in losers get 1 each, even if they go on to make the playoffs.
  • Each draw picks a team weighted by its balls, then takes that team's balls out.
  • A team's own pick can't be #1 two drafts running, or top 5 three drafts running, even when someone else owns it. If a barred team is drawn, it goes to the first slot it is allowed and that slot is drawn again.
  • Picks 17 to 30 go to the other 14 teams, worst record first.
  • Before the season ends, this uses today's standings and assumes the 8 seed loses the 7-vs-8 game. Ties are a coin flip in the real thing; here the weaker team by point differential goes first.
