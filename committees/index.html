Committees

Four committees keep the league running. Each one works from a dashboard — the page that shows what is waiting on it right now. Committee membership is a role, and most dashboards need one; the rosters board is public because nothing on it is private.

Want on one? Committees are staffed by volunteers — say so in Discord, or leave it on the suggestions board. Roles are granted by an admin and every one of them is listed with its exact permissions on Roles & Permissions. The rulebook is what all five committees enforce; where a committee has discretion rather than a rule, the rulebook says so.
