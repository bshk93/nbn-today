The two raw files behind all of this — allstats-{season}.csv and allstats-playoffs-{YY}.csv — are append-only by contract and are the only data on the server that cannot be rebuilt. A row already on disk is corrected with edit_allstats.py, never by hand. A weekly integrity job re-checks row counts, hashes closed seasons, and runs the value-level checks; it reports to Discord rather than here, so a silent week is the good case.
