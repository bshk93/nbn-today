Trade Request Committee

How this works

A team proposes a trade from the tradeblock's "Propose a Trade" builder (or, for reference, the transaction simulator's trade mode), using the "Submit to TRC" button. Every team named in the trade, the proposer's included, must consent here before it opens for a vote. Only a team's owner can consent, and each owner gets an inbox notice when a trade naming their team is submitted. Team members see only their own trades. Three trc members must approve before it's ready; a vote needs a note because it's judging fairness, not legality (legality is checked live, automatically, the whole time a request is open). Only trc_head can reject or finalize a request. Finalizing executes the trade for real — rosters, picks and the transaction ledger update immediately, and it posts to #transactions — so there's no undo once you click it.
