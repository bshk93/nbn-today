Trade Request Committee

Team-proposed trades — consent, ballots, and the final call.

How this works

A team proposes a trade from the tradeblock's "Propose a Trade" builder (or, for reference, the transaction simulator's trade mode), using the "Submit to TRC" button. Every team named in the trade must consent — from the sim, or from the ⋯ menu on their team page — before it opens for ballots. Three trc members must approve before it's ready; a ballot needs a note because it's judging fairness, not legality (legality is checked live, automatically, the whole time a request is open). Only trc_head can reject or finalize a request. Finalizing executes the trade for real — rosters, picks and the transaction ledger update immediately, and it posts to #transactions — so there's no undo once you click it.
